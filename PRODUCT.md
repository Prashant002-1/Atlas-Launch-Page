# Product

## Position

Atlas is an exploration of an AI-native learning management system: how assignments, assessment, and course operations change when AI is part of learning. It is an independent project, not a company or a commercial product.

The sequence is deliberate:

1. AI Assignments
2. Assessment and feedback
3. Full-course coursework and the complete LMS

## AI Assignments

A teacher describes the work in ordinary language. Atlas reads for the learning goal, course material, the role AI may play, and the evidence that should return. Each student works in a course-grounded agent session. The artifact and the process stay connected.

AI Assignments are not an AI detector, a generic chatbot, or a prompt generator. They are a new assignment type designed around problem framing, course grounding, challenge, revision, and teacher review.

## Assessment

Atlas organizes exact claims, revisions, course sources, and final work around the teacher's criteria. It can prepare a draft assessment. The teacher reviews the evidence and decides what becomes final.

## Atlas Course

Atlas Course carries the same context across a full term of assignments, material, feedback, and grades. It is the coursework layer, not a claim that Atlas bundles publisher textbooks or prewritten course content.

## Atlas LMS

Atlas LMS brings courses, materials, modules, people, assignments, grades, integrations, and agents into one learning record. AI participates in the work because it belongs to the system, not because a chat panel was added beside it.

## Connectors

Atlas can run as the LMS. For a course that keeps an existing LMS, roster, role, assignment launch, course material, and teacher-approved grade return are the connection points the design anticipates. None of them are live, and the site must not describe them as if they were.

## Public truth

- Atlas is a project. There is no pilot, no pricing, no service, and nothing for sale.
- The site describes what was built and why. It does not sell.
- No customer logos, usage numbers, testimonials, certifications, or live integrations are claimed.
- The forms collect only what is needed to reply to a visitor. They are not for student records or sensitive course data.
- Anyone who deploys Atlas owns the data, hosting, and approval decisions for their deployment.

## Voice

Direct, specific, and intelligent. The page explains the idea once and lets the visual object carry the rest. Where the author speaks, it is first person singular. There is no "we".
