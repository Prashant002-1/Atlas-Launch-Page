Terms of use

These terms cover the Atlas website, access requests, and free demo pilot. The pilot is a chance to explore Atlas and share feedback. Institutional use is arranged directly with your team.

Exploring Atlas

You are welcome to browse the website and request pilot access to evaluate Atlas. The pilot is free, and pricing will be announced after it ends. Requesting or taking part in the pilot does not commit you to a paid plan.

Getting pilot access

Send an access request and we will follow up by email. Our team provides login credentials when your access is ready. The pilot uses a demo environment with sample data, intended for exploring the features and giving feedback.

Before you begin, we will share your access details and explain how demo data is handled. If you want to use Atlas with your institution’s data, we will agree on scope, privacy, security, and any additional terms with your team first.

Using the site and demo

Use the access credentials provided to you and keep them private. Work with sample data in the demo. Please do not upload real student records, confidential materials, or other people’s personal information.

Do not use Atlas for unlawful activity, impersonation, spam, or unauthorized access, and do not interfere with the service or another person’s use of it. Tell us about bugs or security concerns at contact@atlas-lms.com.

Content and feedback

Atlas retains ownership of its website, software, name, and brand. You retain ownership of the content you provide. Sharing feedback allows us to use your suggestions to improve Atlas.

You may share links to the website. Please ask before using the Atlas brand in a way that suggests a partnership or endorsement.

During the pilot

We may update features or reset sample data as we improve the demo. Use it to evaluate the experience, rather than to store work you need to keep or make official grading decisions. Review AI-generated output before relying on it.

The pilot is provided as available, without a commitment to uninterrupted access. We may limit or end access for misuse or when the pilot ends.

Questions and changes

For questions about these terms, contact contact@atlas-lms.com. Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal information. We may update these terms as Atlas develops; the date above identifies the latest version.
