Before you request access

The questions we expect.

Why are fixed public prices not listed yet?

The first Atlas launches include hands-on course setup and onboarding. The work can differ significantly between one course and a broader institutional rollout. We will publish standard pricing when the product and onboarding paths are stable enough for those numbers to be meaningful.

Who is Atlas being built for?

Atlas is being built for K-12 through undergraduate courses, with a teacher-controlled course agent and a personal Atlas for each student and teacher.

Is Atlas an AI detector?

No. Atlas focuses on the learning journey instead of trying to infer authorship from a finished answer. The teacher sets the terms for agent participation and can review the work that happened inside an assessed session.

Can I request a demonstration without planning a rollout?

Yes. Choose individual interest or product demo in the launch access form and tell us what you want to understand.

What can be configured for a course agent?

The teacher can describe the agent’s name, voice, teaching approach, level of challenge, use of direct answers, grounding in course sources, and what students should know about it.
