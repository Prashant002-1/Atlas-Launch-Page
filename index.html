02The shift

The answer stopped being proof.

Students already work with AI. A finished assignment no longer shows who did the thinking.

So teachers are left to guess, or to police. Neither one is teaching. Atlas changes what the course records in the first place.

What was handed in

Finished, clean, and silent about how it got there.

What actually happened

The route taken — including the turn that went nowhere.

03Governance

Agents that answer to the teacher.

The teacher sets the terms for how AI takes part in their course. Nothing runs outside them.

i

Set the terms.

Every piece of work declares how AI participates. The course decides — not the student, and not the vendor.

ii

Assign an agent.

Put an agent beside a student to work a problem through with them, shaped by the material the teacher gave it.

iii

Agents overseeing agents.

Agents can raise and supervise other agents, all of it held inside the boundaries the course already defines.

04Evidence

Make learning visible.

When a course can see how an answer was reached, assessment stops guessing and teaching gets its attention back.
