Assess the work against your rubric.

Leila teaches first-year composition at a public university. She loves helping students find their own point of view. She cares less about a polished first answer than whether students can read closely, defend a claim, and revise it honestly.

“I want to hear their thinking in the final draft, not a version of what they think I want to hear.”

Her rubric asks three questions.

  1. Make a clear claim.

    Does the student take a specific position that another reader could challenge?

  2. Use the text.

    Does the student support the claim with close reading instead of general summary?

  3. Revise with purpose.

    When the evidence pushes back, does the final draft show what changed and why?

Atlas brings the relevant work to each question. Leila makes the final judgment.
