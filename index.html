Why Atlas exists

AI is already in the assignment. The real choice is who sets its terms.

When AI sits outside the course, students use whatever is available and teachers are left judging the finished answer. Atlas brings the agent into the course. Teachers decide how it teaches and where it stops. Students stay in control as they question, reason, revise, and finish the work. The process becomes visible, so educators can respond to learning instead of policing output.

Stop chasing AI after the fact. Put educators back in control.

The Atlas model

Set the terms. See the learning.

The teacher configures the course agent in plain language. Students work with it inside the assignment. Atlas keeps the questions, hints, revisions, and result together.

One continuous record

From course terms to the next teaching move.

Atlas does not treat the chat, the submission, and the grade as separate events. They remain one learning journey.
