Why Atlas exists

A finished answer no longer tells you who did the work.

Students already use AI. Teachers are left reading the result and guessing what happened before it arrived.

Atlas puts the agent inside the course. The teacher decides what it can do. The student still has to question, reason, and revise. The teacher can see that process and grade the learning, not just the output.
