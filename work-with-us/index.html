Choose the first course

You show us where AI has made the finished answer harder to trust or where students need better support while doing the work.

  • A teacher or course team ready to author the agent terms
  • A clear assignment or learning journey
  • A defined first group of students
Author the teaching terms

We work with the teacher to describe the voice, approach, challenge level, use of direct answers, and student disclosure.

  • Course materials and sources
  • Open study and assessed-work boundaries
  • Teacher review before students enter
Prepare the real course

We bring the selected course structure and materials into Atlas and walk the teacher and student paths before launch.

  • Course setup and selected materials
  • Teacher review of the course-agent record
  • A clear student introduction
Run the first work

Students use their personal Atlas inside the course terms. Teachers see the assessed journey and interpret the evidence.

  • One continuous student session
  • Teacher transcript and rubric review
  • Direct feedback from the course
Review before expanding

We examine what became newly visible, where students needed support, and what the first course says about the next rollout.

  • Changes to agent terms or workflow
  • Teacher and student experience
  • A specific next scope
