Who should reach out

Bring a real learning problem.

The best first conversations are concrete. You know what you want students to learn, what is hard to see today, or which course workflow needs to change.

  • Teacher or professor You want to try an AI assignment or bring a course.
  • Course or program lead You want a shared approach across multiple sections.
  • Academic leader You are evaluating how AI should enter teaching and assessment.
  • Institution team You are considering a complete learning system.

How we will work with you

A short path to something real.

We prepare the environment around your scope. You inspect it before students or institutional records enter the system.

Teacher or course team Assignment or course launch
  1. 01
    Describe the class

    Tell us the course, the learning goal, and the first thing you want to test.

  2. 02
    Inspect a prepared demo

    See the teacher controls, student session, and learning record around your use case.

  3. 03
    Set the terms together

    Bring the relevant materials and decide how the agent may help.

  4. 04
    Review before launch

    Test both sides of the experience, change what is wrong, then choose whether to proceed.

School or college Institutional evaluation
  1. 01
    Form the evaluation group

    Bring academic, administrative, and technical owners into the same conversation.

  2. 02
    Walk the complete system

    Inspect institution setup, course operations, teacher work, student work, and records.

  3. 03
    Define requirements

    Map roles, integrations, policies, data movement, and the representative first scope.

  4. 04
    Prepare a bounded launch

    Configure the environment, review it with your team, and decide on the first cohort.
