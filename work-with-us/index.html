What happens next

A guided test, then a real decision.

  1. First contact

    Tell us who you are and what you want to inspect.

    The short form gives us enough context to prepare the right demo. A use case helps, but it is optional.

    Short request
  2. Test environment

    Explore a complete fictional classroom.

    We give you teacher and student access to a course with materials, assignments, conversations, submissions, and grades. We can walk through it with your teachers or let your team explore on its own.

    No live student data
  3. Fit review

    Decide whether Atlas fits your course and institution.

    If you want to proceed, we bring in the right academic, administrative, compliance, and technical stakeholders. We review requirements, integrations, and the first scope together.

    Your team decides
  4. Real launch

    Prepare the first course.

    We help bring over the course structure and materials, including work from an existing LMS when that is useful. The teacher reviews the agent’s behavior and the student experience before anyone begins.

    One course first

What you can test

Use it. Question it. Bring the people who will judge it.

  • Configure a course agent in plain language
  • Try a governed assignment as a student
  • Review the transcript, final work, and rubric
  • Inspect the teacher and student experience
  • Discuss integrations and institutional requirements
