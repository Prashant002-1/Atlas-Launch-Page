Privacy

This page explains the limited information handled by the Atlas public website and launch access form. Product or pilot data will be governed by separate terms for that engagement.

InformationUseProvidersRetentionChoicesContact

Information we receive

If you submit the launch access form, Atlas receives the information you enter, including your name, email address, role, organization type, institution or organization, course context, requested use case, current approach, and area of interest.

Please do not put student records, confidential course material, credentials, health information, or other sensitive personal data into the public access form.

How we use it

We use access-request information to reply, understand pilot fit, prepare a relevant product conversation, prevent abuse, and keep a record of the correspondence. We do not sell the information submitted through the form.

Site and delivery providers

The website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel Web Analytics provides aggregate page and device information without third-party cookies. It may include the page path, referrer, filtered query parameters, approximate region, browser, operating system, and device type.

The access form sends the submitted information as an email through Resend to the Atlas access inbox. Provider handling is also subject to the providers' own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We retain access requests and related correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to evaluate and manage the relationship, keep operational records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information you submitted through the public form, subject to legal and operational limits. You may also contact us if you no longer want a response to your request.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email contact@atlas-lms.com. We may update this page as the public site, pilot process, or providers change.
