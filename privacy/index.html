Privacy

We use the information you share to arrange access, answer your questions, and support your experience with Atlas. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

InformationUsePilot accessProvidersRetentionChoicesContact

Information you share

When you request access, we receive your name, email, role, organization type, institution or organization if applicable, course or learning context, and what you would like to try. We also receive any information you choose to send us by email.

A short description is enough. Use general examples in the form and sample data in the demo, keeping student records, passwords, and confidential information out of both.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, arrange pilot access, provide support, and follow up on feedback you choose to share. We also use basic website usage information to improve the site and help prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Providers that help us run the website and deliver email process information as described below. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Trying Atlas

Pilot access is free. We provide login credentials for a demo environment with sample data so you can explore the features without uploading real student or institutional records.

We will explain how demo interactions are handled, including retention, deletion, and any AI providers involved, with your access details. For institutional use, we work with your team to agree on privacy, security, and data requirements before you bring real data into Atlas.

Who helps us run the website

Vercel hosts the website. Vercel Web Analytics helps us understand page visits through aggregate information such as page paths, referrers, approximate region, browser, and device type. It does not use cookies to identify individual visitors. Read Vercel’s analytics privacy information.

Access requests are delivered to the Atlas inbox through Resend. If you choose the direct email option, your email service sends the message to us. Hosting and email providers also process the technical information needed to operate their services. See the Vercel and Resend privacy policies.

How long we keep access requests

We keep your request and related correspondence while arranging access and supporting your pilot. You can ask us to delete them at any time. We may need to keep limited records to meet legal obligations or resolve an outstanding issue, and will explain that if it applies to your request.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the personal information you shared with us, correct it, request its deletion, or ask us to stop following up. Email contact@atlas-lms.com and we will help. We may ask you to confirm your identity before sharing or changing personal information.

Questions and updates

Contact contact@atlas-lms.com with privacy questions or requests. We will update this page as our services or data practices change, and keep the date above current.
