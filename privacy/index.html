Privacy

This site explains an open project. The only personal information it handles is what you choose to send through the learn-more or contact form, and it is used to reply to you. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

InformationUseProvidersRetentionChoicesContact

Information you share

When you use the learn-more form, I receive your name, email, role, what you are curious about, your question, and, if you add them, your organization and what you are working on. When you use the contact form, I receive your name, email, selected topic, and message.

A short description is enough. Please keep passwords, student records, and other confidential information out of both forms.

How it is used

Your information is used to reply to you and to follow up on anything you asked about. Basic website usage information helps improve the site and prevent abuse.

Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising. The providers below process what is needed to host the site and deliver email. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

Services behind the site

The site is hosted on Vercel. Form submissions are delivered by Resend to a private inbox. Neither form stores submissions in a database.

Website analytics show aggregate visits, pages, approximate regions, and device types. They do not use cookies to identify individual visitors. See how website analytics work.

How long messages are kept

Your message and any correspondence are kept while the conversation is active. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time. Limited records may be kept where the law requires it, and I will say so if that applies to your request.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the personal information you shared, correct it, request its deletion, or ask me to stop following up. Use the contact form and choose Privacy request. I may ask you to confirm your identity before sharing or changing personal information.

Questions and updates

Send privacy questions or requests through the contact form. This page will be updated if the site or its practices change, and the date above will stay current.
