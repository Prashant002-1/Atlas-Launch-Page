# Atlas product-site design system

## Direction

The site should feel like an intelligent editorial product launch, not a diagram of a software ecosystem.

Each frame gets one decisive visual event. Large type establishes the claim. A real product surface, film, physical artwork, or readable learning record proves it. Negative space is part of the composition.

## Visual hierarchy

- The hero is centered and quiet.
- Small reactive color elements stay inside shallow top and bottom bands.
- The V7 film is the only product film used.
- One original physical artwork represents revision and accumulated evidence.
- The raw instruction, evidence review, and LMS dashboard are concrete product objects.
- Supporting routes use one strong visual or product surface, then direct text.

## Rejected motifs

- solar systems, orbits, connected balls, node graphs, and system maps;
- decorative connector lines and curves;
- fake sliders, switches, dials, or controls;
- generic AI gradients, neon glow, and glossy sci-fi objects;
- rows of tiny uppercase labels that explain visible content;
- fake dashboards, logos, metrics, testimonials, pricing tiers, or compliance badges.

## Labels

Use a label only when it is part of the product or required for navigation, accessibility, a form field, a criterion, a status, or a legal distinction. Do not add a label to name the visual treatment or repeat what the surrounding heading already says.

## Motion

- Motion reveals a state change or responds to the visitor.
- The hero field uses one low-power draw loop and stops when hidden.
- Product films require an explicit play action.
- The assignment reading sequence and assessment tabs have clear user or viewport triggers.
- Reduced motion removes spatial transitions without hiding content.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop compositions may use a strong two-column editorial frame. Mobile should become a clean reading sequence, not a shrunken diagram. The LMS dashboard may scroll inside its own bounded preview. The page itself must not overflow.

## Inspiration ritual

Before a major visual pass, inspect current work from sources such as DialKit, Learn UI, Learn Kernels, Learn Inference, Canvas UI, Recent Design, Collect UI, 60fps, and Design Engineer Tools. Keep only a technique that improves Atlas. Do not import another site's palette, motif, or component chrome.
